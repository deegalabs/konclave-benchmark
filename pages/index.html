Konclave · Milestone 1 review

Konclave benchmark

What scanning and proving a Zcash shielded wallet cost in this browser, on this device. Nothing to install. A proof row and a one-day scan row take from about 6 minutes on a high-end phone to about an hour on an entry phone.

  1. 1

    Copy the published test key

    A view-only key of a test vault, published in the repository (config/test-vault.json). It cannot spend. Never paste a key of your own.

  2. 2

    Open the review build

    Its header must show "Harness wasm 656e967 ... page script 656e967".

    Open the build (656e967)
  3. 3

    Run a row

    Paste the key, type https://zec.global (run by Zingo Labs) as the endpoint, and tap Run.

    Compare the median with the class of your device in the reviewer sheet. A row through zec.global is information, not a verdict: the verdict rows went through the project's proxies.

What leaves your device

Results stay in the browser until you press "Copy all"; nothing is sent to us.

A scan row calls the light client server you type: that server sees your IP address and the range of blocks you ask for, as it would for any wallet.

Details for checking the build

The build is the web/ folder of the release zip konclave-benchmark-656e967.zip, published unchanged after its sha256 was checked (5aa53dc4a8a589a9b384be650c2262189e9088886bf6a576315f2bf199070165) and every file compared with the zip.

The main page runs on one thread, as the verdict rows did. Its "Threads page" link tries to turn threads on with a service worker, since this host cannot send the isolation headers; the "Mode:" line in its header says whether it worked (on the A72's Chrome it did not).

Source, method and results: github.com/deegalabs/konclave-benchmark.
